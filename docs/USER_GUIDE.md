# PASTI Assets — User Guide

For IT, HR/Admin and Security staff. No training needed beyond this page.

## Signing in
Open the link IT gave you and sign in with your email and password.
First time? Use the link in your invitation email to set a password.
Forgot it? Type your email and click **Forgot password?**.

If you see **“Waiting for approval”**, ask an IT administrator to give you a role.

## The menu
| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | What needs attention today. Click a card or a ⚠ line to go straight to it. |
| **Asset Register** | Every company device and its permanent asset code. |
| **Employees** | Who holds what; print accountability forms; exit clearance. |
| **Gate Passes** | Device pass badges (valid 3 months). |
| **Gate Log** | Security checks devices in and out. |
| **Personal Devices** | Employee-owned laptops/phones brought to the office (BYOD). |
| **Accessories** | Chargers, bags, mice, headsets handed out. |
| **Assignment History** | Who held each device, from when to when. |
| **Activity Log** | Every change, with who did it and when. |

A yellow number next to a menu item means something there needs action.

## Everyday tasks

### Register a new device
**Asset Register → Register asset.** Fill in the date, device type, brand/model and the serial number
*exactly* as printed on the label. The asset code is created when you save. If you are handing it over
today, choose the employee in **Issue to**.
If the serial is already registered, the form tells you which record has it — open that one instead.

### Give a device to someone / move it to another person
Open the asset → **Issue to employee** (or **Hand over** if someone already has it).
The previous holder's record is closed and their gate pass is revoked automatically.

### A device comes back to IT
Open the asset → **Return / retire** → *Returned to IT*, the date and its condition.
Before re-issuing: back up what the company needs, wipe/reimage, set up the admin and guest accounts.
Lost? Choose *Lost/Stolen*. Encoded by mistake? Choose *Void* and say why. Records are never deleted.

### Issue a gate pass
Laptops, phones and tablets in use need one (the Asset Register shows **⚠ Needs gate pass**).
Open the asset → **Issue gate pass**, or **Gate Passes → Issue gate pass**. The pass is in the name of
the person holding the device and is valid for 3 months.

### Renew or replace a pass
**Gate Passes** → find it → **Renew**. Choose *Expired / renewal* or *Old badge lost or damaged*.
The Badge ID stays the same. If the device now belongs to someone else, issue a **new** pass instead.

### At the gate (Security)
**Gate Log** → type the Badge ID from the pass. The box on the right shows:
- **ALLOW** (green) — check the serial on the device matches, then click **Log OUT** or **Log IN**.
- **DO NOT ALLOW** (red) — the pass is revoked, expired or unknown. Do not let the device through.
- **CHECK WITH IT** (amber) — the device has moved to someone else or was returned. Call IT.

### Accountability form
**Employees** → open the person → **Print accountability form**. Have them sign it, then click
**Record signed form** and enter the date. Items issued after that date show **NEW** until a new form is signed.

### Someone leaves the company
Edit the employee → Employment status *Resigned/Separated* and the date. **Exit clearance** lists every
device, accessory and gate pass still to collect, and shows **Cleared ✓** when everything is back.

### Accessories
**Accessories → Record accessory.** One row per hand-out. When it comes back, click **Returned**.

### Export to Excel
Every list has **Export to Excel**. It exports exactly what you see — apply your filters first.

## What the warnings mean
| Warning | What to do |
|---|---|
| ⚠ Needs gate pass | Issue a gate pass for the device. |
| ⚠ Placeholder serial | Replace it with the real serial from the device label (Edit details). |
| ⚠ Brand/Model missing | Edit details and fill it in. |
| ⚠ Expires in N day(s) / EXPIRED | Renew the pass. |
| ⚠ Device now with … – revoke & issue new badge | Revoke the old pass and issue one for the new holder. |
| ⚠ N new item(s) – print & sign | Print the form, have it signed, record the date. |
| ⚠ Not cleared | Collect the listed items from the departing employee. |

## Problems?
- *“Your account is not allowed to do that”* — your role is read-only. Ask an administrator.
- *“Could not reach the server”* — check the internet connection and try again; nothing was saved.
- Something looks wrong in a record — check **Activity Log** to see who changed it and when.
